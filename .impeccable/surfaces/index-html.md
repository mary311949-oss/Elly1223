# Surface: Carrel 座位預約 Prototype (index.html)

Mode: Operate. Audience: university students on phones (and laptops) booking a library study seat in fixed slots, then checking in within 15 minutes. Task, rules and states come from PRODUCT.md and SHAPE.md.

User-chosen direction: reference foreign university library booking systems (Cambridge Spacefinder, LibCal family, Bodleian / UL reading-room plans); user locked "閱覽室平面圖" (the reading-room plan direction). Reference sites could not be fetched from this environment; analysis rests on prior knowledge.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The floor is the interface. Seat choice happens on an architect's reading-room plan, not on a grid of coloured squares; the category default (LibCal's green/grey cell grid) is refused for seats and kept only as plain slot cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink-navy college shell (rail / top bar), cool pale stone ground, white paper sheets. Plan drawn in ink: thick poché walls, double-line windows, door swing arcs, hatched bookstacks. College green = available / primary action; booked seats carry plan hatching; my seat is solid green with a check. Public Sans + Noto Sans TC, tabular numerals for times and seat IDs.

STORY: Student sees their next booking and check-in deadline first, picks date and slot, walks the building directory to a floor, chooses a desk on the plan knowing power / accessibility / zone, reads the rules, confirms, later checks in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: navy shell; next-booking sheet with mini floor plan locating the seat and check-in window; quick-book row for the next open slot with seats left. Booking step 2: building-directory floor list (4F/3F/2F stacked like a wall sign) left, plan centre, selection sheet right (bottom sheet on phone).
